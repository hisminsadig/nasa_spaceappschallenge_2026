Challenge Name

Create health monitoring software for astronauts on space missions.

Presentation

WallkiHealth means “Health Companion.” Its name is inspired by Wallki, a word associated with companionship in the Ancash variety of Central Quechua, an Indigenous language of Peru.

WallkiHealth is an educational, AI-assisted platform designed to support astronaut health during long-duration space missions. It integrates daily vital-sign monitoring, health records, personalized preventive guidance, medical follow-up, and early alerts into one accessible system.

By monitoring heart rate, oxygen levels, blood pressure, sleep, stress, and radiation exposure, WallkiHealth helps astronauts and medical teams identify potential health changes and make informed decisions.

Developed independently in Peru, WallkiHealth combines artificial intelligence, technology, and Andean cultural inspiration with one purpose: to be a trusted health companion for astronauts beyond Earth.
